Why Choose Us

Features

Everything you need for a comfortable and productive hostel life at FATA University.

High-Speed Internet

High Speed Internet Facility is available in the hostel. Wi-Fi is available 24/7 in the entire hostel premises, including rooms, common areas, and study halls.

Mess Facility

A well-managed mess is available to provide hygienic and nutritious food to the students. The mess operates on a buffet system, offering a variety of dishes for breakfast, lunch, and dinner.

Study Zone

Every hostel rooms have a study table and Chair, in all hostel rooms there are almari for cloths.

Student-Friendly Hostel

Inclusive and welcoming community for students from all departments of FATA University.

24/7 Security

There is a very secure enviroment for the students, there is a guard 24/7 at the gate of the hostel.

Laundry Services

In Faqir Ipi House the laundry service is available. In Fatima House and Khyber House the laundry service is not available.
